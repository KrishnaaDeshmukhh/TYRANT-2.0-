# TYRANT Reboot — Distribution

Status: EXPLORING

## Primary Arena

TYRANT is social-media-first. Short-form distribution is the primary initial target.

## Candidate Platforms

Previously discussed platforms include:
- YouTube Shorts
- Instagram Reels
- Facebook
- Threads
- TikTok
- Reddit
- Bluesky
- Snapchat Spotlight

The exact launch set and account/channel topology remain open.

## Distribution Principle

Distribution should be treated as a reusable capability shared by content formats. A content asset should be routable to appropriate channels rather than being permanently tied to one platform.

## Current Portfolio Implications

- Clipping can publish transformed short-form assets.
- Storytelling and animated explainers can be produced primarily for short-form feeds.
- List/recommendation content can route to platforms where product discovery and affiliate conversion are useful.
- Longer source assets may be created when they can feed later clipping/repurposing.

## Open

Final platform set, account/channel structure, publishing cadence, scheduling strategy, platform-specific packaging, and distribution experiments remain undecided.
