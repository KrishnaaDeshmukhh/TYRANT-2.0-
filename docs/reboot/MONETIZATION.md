# TYRANT Reboot — Monetization

Status: EXPLORING

## Current Revenue Directions

### Affiliate Commerce — PROPOSED / HIGH PRIORITY

Best current fit for List / Ranking / Recommendation content and potentially product/review content.

Potential categories include AI tools, SaaS, software, technology, gadgets, and other products with affiliate programs.

Core model:

```text
Useful recommendation content
→ product/tool discovery
→ affiliate link / product tag
→ click
→ conversion
→ commission
```

The content must provide genuine selection and comparison value. Affiliate monetization should not turn TYRANT into indiscriminate product spam.

### Creator Clipping Service — PROPOSED

TYRANT can sell clipping/repurposing as a service to creators, potentially charging for delivered clips and offering a higher-priced package that includes distribution through TYRANT-owned channels.

### Owned Social Media — PROPOSED

Publish TYRANT-created content on owned channels and monetize through platform revenue, sponsorships, affiliate commerce, and other streams as eligibility and performance develop.

### Sponsorships — EXPLORING

Potentially relevant once a channel or format has enough audience and commercial relevance. Not a prerequisite for initial operation.

## Monetization Principle

Revenue should be considered at content-format selection time. Formats with a natural commercial intent, such as recommendations and buying guides, have an advantage because the path from content to revenue is direct.

## Open

Exact affiliate networks, revenue targets, sponsorship model, pricing for clipping services, channel economics, and attribution implementation remain to be decided.
